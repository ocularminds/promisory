# ADR 0018: Retention, personal-data reporting and administrator export

Status: accepted for implementation; scheduled jobs still need live verification.

- **Storage:** `RawStore.remove(scope, kind, id, expectedRevision)` is one guarded `DELETE … AND revision = ?`. Zero affected rows is a `CONFLICT`, so a record changed by a concurrent writer is never deleted. Multi-statement transactions are still not assumed.
- **Retention:** `configSchema.retentionDays` is now administrator-settable (30–365, default 180). The hourly job deletes a commitment record only after the period has passed since closure (cancelled or superseded), verification (fulfilled) or, for an abandoned draft, its last activity. Records with any open or reconciling operation, or a pending finalization, are never deleted: indiscriminate cleanup must not turn uncertainty into a new dispatch (privacy disclosure). Signed Decionis evidence and the external journal follow their own retention.
- **Personal data:** a daily scheduled trigger reports every account ID in stored account fields (`*AccountId`, binding `accountId`, pending-owner `proposedBy`, inbox keys; system principals and pseudonyms excluded) through `privacy.reportPersonalData` in batches of 90. Closed accounts get a deterministic pseudonym (`deleted-user-<16 hex>`) in every account field of commitments, project records and configuration, and their inbox record is deleted. Structure, decisions and timestamps stay, so history remains explainable without identifying the person.
- **Export:** `data.export` (project administrator) returns the selected project's Promisory records from the same access-filtered workspace read used by every view. It excludes restricted commitments and Teamwork Graph context.
- **Distribution:** registering the judge and Marketplace identities, sharing links and submitting a listing are owner actions. They are prepared in `docs/submission/judge-and-marketplace-distribution.md`, which recommends judge-app installs plus guest access to the bound test site for the successful governed write.
