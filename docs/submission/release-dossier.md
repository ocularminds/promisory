## Prepared product copy

**Name:** Promisory  
**Tagline:** Keep every commitment connected.  
**Category proposal:** Apps for Software Teams  
**Short description:** Connect explicit commitments to Jira work, accountable people and verifiable outcomes. Follow dependencies, review exact recovery proposals and keep the original promise visible.

**Long description draft:**

Promisory gives a delivery team a shared view of what it has agreed to deliver, who has accepted accountability, when the outcome is due and which evidence will establish success. It connects those agreements to Jira's actual work hierarchy and dependencies, alongside selected Confluence strategy, discovery, requirements, readiness, release and learning artifacts.

When work or requirements change, Promisory explains affected commitments through typed paths and explicit source versions. Missing access and stale evidence remain visible. Owners accept agreement changes separately from engineering schedule edits, so moving a Jira date does not silently renegotiate a promise.

The release includes Rovo read/proposal tools backed by the same domain service as the workspace, permission-filtered Teamwork Graph context, and one optional controlled action: a reviewed Jira due-date change through a configured Decionis/AgentSafe execution boundary. Enabling that action requires a separately configured remote, institutional policy and verified enforcement. A review, readiness gate, authorization, native write and fulfilled outcome remain distinct records.

**Publication condition:** The development installation has live Rovo/graph observations and a verified normal governed action, with the limits recorded above. Complete the remaining acceptance cases and fresh judge/Marketplace installations before presenting these paragraphs as claims for those release profiles. Keep fixture screenshots visibly labelled and never describe them as connected customer data.
